# My Favorite Series: Love in Disguise (2026)

## About

**Love in Disguise** is a South Korean romantic comedy and suspense thriller series that premiered in October 2026. I love it because of its interesting storyline, unexpected chemistry, and the perfect balance between romance, comedy, and mystery.

It's basically about two people hiding secrets, one pretending to be someone she's not, and feelings getting involved when they least expect it. LIKE??? The tension is everything.

> "Two people hiding their identities, yet somehow becoming the reason for each other's happiness."

---

## Main Characters

1. **Yoon Yi-jun (Yim Si-wan)** – A wealthy chaebol heir who becomes a murder suspect. He's arrogant, stubborn, and honestly, such an interesting character.
2. **Kang Jae-hee (Seol In-ah)** – A former special forces officer turned detective who disguises herself as a male secretary to investigate Yi-jun. She's smart, brave, and knows how to handle herself.
3. **Kim Si-hyun (Kim Jung-hyun)** – Yi-jun's longtime best friend and a lawyer who works for Taegang Group. He's one of the characters who adds more depth to the story.
